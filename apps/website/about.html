Our Mission

We believe technology should enhance family life, not dominate it. ScreenSprout was founded to help parents guide their children toward healthy digital habits through positive reinforcement and smart tools.

Our Story

ScreenSprout began in 2023 when a group of parents realized that existing screen time solutions were either too restrictive or too complicated. We wanted something different: a tool that would help families find balance while respecting children's growing independence.

What started as a simple time-tracking app has grown into a comprehensive platform used by thousands of families worldwide. Our approach combines behavioral science, child development research, and real parent feedback to create tools that actually work.

Today, ScreenSprout is more than just a monitoring tool—it's a partnership between parents and children to build lifelong healthy habits around technology use.
